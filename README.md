### 🧠 RetailSense Decision Engine
*A retail merchandising decision-support system*

Retail chains often rely on general rules like “bigger stores sell more”.  
RetailSense analyzes store and product behavior to identify what actually drives performance.

Instead of predicting sales, the system helps planners decide:
- where to allocate inventory
- which stores suit a product category
- whether visibility or location matters more

**Demonstrates:** translating model outputs into operational decisions.

---

### 📉 Customer Churn Decision System
*Designing interventions based on churn risk*

Many churn models stop at probability scores.  
This project focuses on how businesses should act once risk is identified.

The system prioritizes customers into action tiers such as:
- immediate retention outreach
- engagement campaigns
- passive monitoring

The emphasis is on operational clarity — not predictive accuracy.

**Demonstrates:** turning predictive signals into intervention strategy.

---

### 🧩 Customer Value Segmentation (RFM)
*Behavior-based customer prioritization*

Businesses often see customer metrics but struggle to decide who deserves attention.  
This case study converts behavioral segmentation into action categories:

- retain immediately
- nurture for growth
- maintain
- low-priority

Instead of presenting analytics, the system guides business prioritization.

**Demonstrates:** converting analytics into clear business actions.
